Honest limits

Evidence-based insights, not absolute proof

EcoLens provides evidence-based verification and confidence insights, but does not replace field audits, local communities, or human verification. Our goal is to bring transparency, consistency, and early warning reality gap detection to environmental commitments.

Vision

A trust layer for environmental pledges

EcoLens aims to make environmental commitments more transparent, measurable, and accountable through evidence-based verification.
