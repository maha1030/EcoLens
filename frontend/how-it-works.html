How it works

From promise to early warning

The exact pipeline EcoLens runs for every registered project.

Environmental promise

An organization registers a pledge: target, deadline and GPS site.

Progress updates

Monthly submissions with photos, GPS location, date and measurements.

Evidence verification

Each submitted photo, location and progress update goes through multiple verification checks.

Expected vs actual

Verified progress is compared with the promised trajectory: the reality gap.

Prediction & early warning

The trend is forecast to the deadline so failure is visible early, not after.
