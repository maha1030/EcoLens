The problem

Promises are loud. Evidence is silent.

Environmental pledges are everywhere, but almost nobody can check them. Reports are self-declared, photos are reused, and by the time a failure is obvious, the money is spent.

🌳 Pledges everywhere

Governments, companies and NGOs announce thousands of tree-planting, restoration and waste-reduction pledges every year.

❓ No easy way to check

Progress reports are a number and maybe a photo. Hard to audit, easy to inflate, and nobody cross-checks the evidence.

📉 Trust erodes

When pledges quietly fail, funders and citizens stop believing all of them. That is the greenwashing trap.

How do we solve the evidence silence?

EcoLens introduces an automated evidence verification pipeline and early warning reality gap detection.
