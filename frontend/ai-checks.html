The AI checks

Every update is interrogated, not trusted

EcoLens applies multiple verification checks to submitted evidence to identify inconsistencies and improve confidence in reported environmental progress.

Photo relevance

Image analysis evaluates whether a submitted photo appears relevant to the environmental project and its reported activity.

Duplicate detection

Detects potentially reused or duplicate images by comparing submitted evidence with previous project records.

GPS cross-check

Compares submitted location information with the registered project area to identify possible location mismatches.

Growth plausibility

Analyzes reported progress against historical trends to identify unusually large or unrealistic changes.

Anomaly detection

Detects unusual patterns, sudden jumps, or unexpected stalls across project updates.

Confidence score

Combines verification results into a confidence score that indicates how strongly the submitted evidence supports the reported progress.

Honest limits

What AI can and cannot prove here

The goal of EcoLens is transparency and auditability, not proof. We say that on the page, on purpose.

✅ It can

  • Catch photos that were recycled from earlier updates or other projects.
  • Catch growth claims that are implausible against the project's own history.
  • Check that submissions come from the promised location.
  • Attach an honest confidence level to every report.
  • Warn months in advance when the verified trend will miss the promise.

🚫 It cannot

  • Prove a photo was taken on the date claimed (no trusted camera signal).
  • Confirm planted trees survived without periodic field audits.
  • Stop a determined fraudster who submits many genuine photos slowly.
  • Replace on-ground verification by auditors and local communities.
